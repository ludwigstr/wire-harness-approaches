Method details

Identify a simulator from the real clips, generate synthetic data from it, and train an interaction network + bidirectional LSTM along the chain. We use NVIDIA Newton instead of the paper's AGX Dynamics.

Pipeline

  1. Identification (yang_identify.py --engine newton): differential evolution on the one-step error (Eq. 1) over k_stretch, d_stretch, k_bend, d_bend, k_twist, d_twist, mass per metre and μ, on all evaluation clips.
  2. Simulator control: the identified simulator alone on the clips.
  3. Synthetic data: real mover paths with speed-only variation ×0.65–1.4 (one shared time warp), parameters randomised around the identified set. Built from the 14 clips: 1,305 rollouts (95 rejected), 246k transitions.
  4. Training: IN + bi-LSTM, validation split per rollout. A transition is dropped only if its stretch gap exceeds that of its clip's real-timing rollout by more than 10 mm, so violations the real recording already has are tolerated.
  5. Evaluation: 5-frame warm-up, open loop, ends pinned.

Additions to the paper

  • Domain randomisation, speed-only motion variation, and the tolerant stretch-gap filter. Without the filter, the soft identified stretch dropped 71.9 % of the transitions; with it, 93.9 % are kept.
